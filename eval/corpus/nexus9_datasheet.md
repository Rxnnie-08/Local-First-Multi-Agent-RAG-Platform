# Nexus-9 Industrial Drone: Datasheet

The Nexus-9 is a fictional heavy-lift inspection drone used in the evaluation corpus.

## Flight

The Nexus-9 carries up to 9 kilograms and flies for 38 minutes on a full battery. Its maximum
speed is 62 kilometres per hour.

## Battery warranty

The Nexus-9 battery carries a limited 24-month replacement warranty covering thermal degradation
past 30%. Damage from improper storage is not covered.
