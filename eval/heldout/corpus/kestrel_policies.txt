Kestrel Gateway: limits and retention policy

Rate limits
The free tier allows 600 requests per minute per API key.
The business tier allows 6000 requests per minute per API key.
A client over its limit receives HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header.

Request size
The maximum request body is 8 MB. Larger bodies are rejected with HTTP 413.

Data retention
Access logs are retained for 45 days.
Audit records for administrative changes are retained for 730 days.
Metrics are kept at one-minute resolution for 14 days and then downsampled to one hour.

Support
Priority incidents are acknowledged within 15 minutes on the business tier.
The free tier has community support only and no response-time commitment.

Maintenance
Planned maintenance happens on the first Tuesday of each month between 02:00 and 03:00 UTC.
